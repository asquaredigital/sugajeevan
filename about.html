About Us

Sugajeevan Multispeciality Hospital

At Sugajeevan Multispeciality Hospital, we are driven by one guiding principle — “Healing with Compassion and Excellence.” Located at Melavasthachavadi, Thanjavur, our 100-bedded facility ensures that world-class medical care is always within your reach.

We are committed to delivering comprehensive, patient-focused healthcare through a strong culture of teamwork among our medical and non-medical professionals.

Our hospital operates 24×7, providing advanced diagnostic and therapeutic services supported by state-of-the-art infrastructure, modern technology, and highly qualified, dedicated staff.

Our goal is to combine medical expertise with empathy, ensuring every patient receives the highest standards of treatment, comfort, and care.

Patient Accommodation & Facilities

At Sugajeevan Multispeciality Hospital, we provide well-designed and sophisticated patient accommodations to suit the needs and comfort of every individual.

Our facilities include Intensive Care Units, Labour Ward, General Ward, Sharing AC Rooms, Single AC Rooms, and AC Suite Rooms — each equipped with essential healthcare amenities such as oxygen supply, suction points, nurse call system, and electrical provisions for ventilators, monitors, and pulse oximeters.

Every unit is thoughtfully designed to ensure patient safety, hygiene, and convenience, supported by dedicated and trained medical and nursing staff available round-the-clock.

Vision of Sugajeevan

To be the hospital of choice for patients and families by meeting the comprehensive healthcare needs of the community with compassion, commitment, and excellence.

Mission Statement of Sugajeevan

To provide quality patient care with utmost safety, compassion, and integrity, supported by advanced medical technology and a team of dedicated healthcare professionals — ensuring that every patient receives the very best in healthcare services.

  • Service Excellence: We are committed to exceeding expectations through continuous improvement and patient-centered care.
