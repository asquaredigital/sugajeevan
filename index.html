About Us

Let's make an appointment easily

Sugajeevan Speciality Hospital, Thanjavur is a multi-speciality healthcare centre committed to providing comprehensive medical services at affordable costs. With expert consultants across orthopaedics, urology, nephrology, neurosurgery, general surgery, obstetrics, gynaecology, and more, the hospital ensures high-quality treatment supported by modern facilities and compassionate care.

The hospital offers 24/7 emergency and trauma care, ICU services, advanced diagnostic imaging, dialysis, physiotherapy, pharmacy, and preventive health check-ups. Conveniently located on Nagapattinam By-Pass Road, Sugajeevan Speciality Hospital stands as a trusted destination for holistic healthcare, delivering “The Healing Touch” to the community.
